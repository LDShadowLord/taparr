Versioned Tape Header Compatibility

            

            

              Every cartridge initialized by Taparr writes a 1 KB JSON/text descriptor at Filemark 0 (fm0)
              containing both taparr_version and tape_format_version. When future updates introduce
              new manifest fields or blocking capabilities, Taparr automatically inspects fm0 to preserve backward compatibility.
